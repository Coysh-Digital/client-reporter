Introduction
Here’s your website report for August. It was a strong month across the board — store revenue more than doubled and both search and paid channels grew. As always, if anything here raises a question, just reply and we’ll talk it through.
Month in review
AI summary During the reporting period from 1 to 31 August 2026, harbourline.co.uk had a standout month commercially. Store revenue rose to £2,114.42 from £952.19 and orders doubled to 12, while organic search grew to 1,284 clicks (up 18.3%) and the Google Ads account returned £2,180 in tracked sales from £640 of spend — a 3.4× return. The August email campaign reached 4,820 subscribers at a 38.2% open rate. Website visitors rose to 630 from 594, though page views eased to 1,616 and the bounce rate climbed to 49.4%. The site held excellent uptime at 99.79% with no incidents, and Core Web Vitals stayed healthy with a good LCP of 2.3 seconds.
